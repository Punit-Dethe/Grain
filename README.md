Grain lets you talk instead of type, anywhere on your computer.

Press a key, say what you want to write, and your words appear in whatever app you're already using: email, chat, documents, code, a search box. No copying and pasting, no switching windows.

On top of that, Grain can tidy up what you said, take spoken requests like a personal assistant, and record and summarise your meetings. Speech recognition runs on your own computer, so your voice stays with you.

## What's inside

Grain does a few different jobs. Most people start with one and grow into the rest.

| If you want to…                                     | Start with                         |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Write faster than you type                          | [Dictation](#dictation)            |
| Turn rough speech into polished writing             | [AI cleanup](#ai-cleanup-optional) |
| Ask for things out loud: rewrite, explain, look up  | [Agent](#agent)                    |
| Capture meetings and calls without taking notes     | [Recall](#recall-meetings)         |
| Let the Agent work with your browser, the web, apps | [Extensions](#extensions)          |
| Teach Grain your names, phrases and writing style   | [Personalize](#personalize)        |

Everything beyond plain dictation is optional. Turn on what helps you and ignore the rest; switched-off features don't run in the background.

## Dictation

The heart of Grain. One shortcut, from any app.

- **Hold or toggle.** Hold the key while you talk, or press once to start and again to stop. Your choice.
- **Fast, even for long dictation.** With models like Parakeet, a ten-minute brain dump lands moments after you stop talking, not minutes later.
- **Live text for models that support it.** Some models show your words as you speak them.
- **Runs on your computer.** Choose a speech model from the built-in catalogue and Grain downloads it once. If you prefer, you can use a cloud speech service you've set up instead.
- **"Scrap that."** Lost your train of thought? Say "scrap that" and everything before it is thrown away while you keep talking.
- **Filler word removal.** Drops the "um"s and "uh"s.
- **Never lose what you said.** If there was no text box to type into, Grain offers to copy your words so you can paste them yourself.
- **Transcribe a file.** Got an existing recording? Grain can turn it into text too.
- **History.** Every dictation is saved, searchable by date. If AI rewrote it, you can still see exactly what you originally said.

_Who it's for:_ anyone who writes a lot. It's especially handy for long emails, notes, journaling, documentation, and people for whom typing is slow or painful.

## AI cleanup (optional)

Connect an AI provider and Grain can polish your words before they're typed out: fixing grammar, adding structure, matching the tone you need.

- **Decide at the end.** You don't have to choose up front. Start talking normally; when you finish, stop with the AI key to get the polished version, or the normal key to keep your exact words. Or send everything through AI by default.
- **Tell it what to do with your words (Prompt Record).** Mid-dictation, press the Prompt Record key and speak an instruction. _Describe a bug out loud, then say "turn this into a GitHub issue."_ Grain treats the first part as the material and the second as the instruction.
- **Fits where you're writing (Context awareness).** Grain notices which app you're dictating into and adjusts. Dictate in your email and it sounds like an email; in your code editor it stays technical; in a chat app it stays casual. The Email, Work, Casual, Technical and AI chat styles are all editable, and you can make your own for specific apps.
- **Backup providers.** Add more than one provider and Grain falls back to the next if one is unavailable.

_Who it's for:_ people who think out loud but want the result to read like they sat down and wrote it.

## Agent

A voice assistant that lives on your desktop, not in a browser tab.

Press the Agent key and ask for something. Select text first and the Agent works on it: _"make this sound more confident," "translate this," "summarise this."_ With nothing selected, just ask a question.

- **Answers appear in a small notch** at the top of your screen, out of the way of your work. Open it into a full chat to keep the conversation going.
- **See my screen (optional).** Let the Agent look at the window you're in, so you can ask about what you're looking at.
- **Read replies aloud (optional).** Replies spoken in a natural voice generated on your computer. Choose from several voices.
- **Interactive answers.** Some replies come as small cards (a weather card, a list of places) instead of a wall of text. Turn this off if you prefer plain replies.
- **More with extensions.** Give the Agent tools such as web search or your browser. See [Extensions](#extensions).

_Who it's for:_ anyone who'd like a quick helper one keypress away, for rewriting, explaining, answering, and small tasks.

## Recall (meetings)

Record meetings, calls and lectures, and get a transcript and notes afterwards.

- **Records your voice and the other side.** Captures your microphone, your computer's audio, or both. Pause and resume any time.
- **Transcribed on your computer** once you stop, automatically if you like.
- **Who said what.** Grain can label different speakers (a one-time download).
- **Summaries.** One click (or automatically) turns a transcript into notes: what was discussed, what was decided, and who said what. Grain can also name each meeting from what was said.
- **Use your meetings in other AI apps.** Optionally let apps like Claude search and read your meeting notes.

_Who it's for:_ people in lots of calls, students, interviewers, anyone who's tired of choosing between listening and taking notes.

## Extensions

Extensions give the Agent extra abilities. They're optional, and you're asked before the Agent does anything that changes something on your behalf.

- **Web search.** Look things up and read pages.
- **Grain Pilot.** Lets the Agent work in your web browser through a companion browser extension.
- **Grain Recall.** Lets the Agent search your meeting history.
- **Your apps.** Connect services like GitHub, Notion, Linear, calendars and many more from a built-in catalogue, usually in one click.
- **Bring your own.** Advanced users can add any compatible tool server (MCP).

_Who it's for:_ people who want the Agent to actually get things done, not just talk.

## Personalize

- **Dictionary.** Add names, product terms and jargon so Grain spells them right. When you correct a word after dictating, Grain can offer to remember it.
- **Snippets.** Say a short phrase and Grain types saved text in its place. _Say "my address" and your full address appears._
- **Context awareness.** Choose how your writing should sound in each app (see [AI cleanup](#ai-cleanup-optional)).
- **The pill.** A small indicator shows while you're recording, with the icon of the app you're dictating into. You can change how it looks or hide it.
- **Shortcuts, sounds, light and dark mode,** start with your computer, tray icon, and how long history is kept are all in Settings.

## Private by default

- Speech recognition happens on your computer. Your voice isn't uploaded unless you choose a cloud speech service.
- AI features only use providers you set up yourself, and only when you use them.
- Features you switch off actually switch off: no hidden background work, no wasted memory.

## Help shape where Grain goes

Grain is at a very early stage, and its direction isn't set in stone. What gets built next depends a lot on what people actually want.

If there's something you wish Grain did, something that gets in your way, or a direction you think it should take, we'd genuinely love to hear it. [Open an issue](https://github.com/Punit-Dethe/Grain/issues) and tell us. Big ideas are welcome; we're open to taking Grain somewhere quite different if it's the right call.

## Getting started

1. Download Grain from [Releases](https://github.com/Punit-Dethe/Grain/releases).
2. Allow microphone access, and accessibility/input access if your computer asks for it. Grain needs this to type into other apps.
3. Pick a speech model. Grain downloads it for you.
4. Set your dictation shortcut, click into any text box, and start talking.
5. Optional: connect an AI provider under **Settings → AI** to unlock AI cleanup, the Agent and meeting summaries.

### Good to know

- **An older clipboard item gets pasted instead of your words:** open Grain, press `Ctrl+Shift+D` to show the Debug settings, then try **Reliable Paste** or increase **Paste Delay (After)**. If it keeps happening, [let us know](https://github.com/Punit-Dethe/Grain/issues) which app you were typing into.

## For developers

```bash
bun install
bun run tauri dev
```
