# Grain v0.0.11 — Windows

This release adds Recall, more flexible recording shortcuts, audio-file transcription, and a refreshed Overview.

## New

- **Recall:** record meetings with separate microphone and system-audio controls, generate speaker-labelled transcripts, and review saved recordings with word-synchronised playback and highlighting.
- **Audio-file transcription:** choose a file under **Settings → Application → Storage** and save its transcript to History. Imports show progress, support cancellation, and do not paste into another application.
- **Hold or toggle:** tap your recording shortcut to keep recording, or hold it and release to stop. The existing toggle and hold-only choices remain available under **Settings → Transcription → Recording**.

## Recording and speech models

- Included Handy improvements for shortcut handling, onboarding, model downloads, and transcription. Shortcut presses received while a transcription is finishing are handled more reliably.
- Refreshed the speech-model catalogue and improved model-cache handling.
- Improved onboarding completion and restart behaviour, plus download speed/progress reporting.
- Improved interface responsiveness during clipboard paste and restoration.
- Added separate Traditional Chinese output choices for Taiwan and Hong Kong.

## Extensions and interface

- Carries forward the extension sidebar and installation fixes from the refreshed v0.0.10 Windows installer.
- Removed the unused embedding-based tool-discovery workflow and obsolete extension-platform machinery.
- Refined the Overview hero, artwork, colours, spacing, and tagline.
- Moved Recall below Extensions and directly above Settings in the sidebar.

## Known limitations

- Recall can miss a spoken segment, including a final turn near the end of a recording. Speaker labels can also be imperfect; review the recording when checking a transcript.
- The extension platform remains in beta.
